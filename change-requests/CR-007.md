# CR-007: Match the owner's product form to the product page, add Specifications, and clearer stock messages

Requested by the store owner. Written in the owner's terms. How to build it is up to you.

## 1. The owner can edit everything the product page shows

Today a shopper's product page shows a "Materials & care" list and a line about the maker, but the owner's product form has no way to edit either. A product created in the owner area always has an empty Materials & care list.

The owner's product form must let the owner edit, for each product:

- **Materials & care**: a list, one item per line.
- **Maker line**: the short italic line under the description (for example "Thrown by Ines in the studio's back room"). Optional. When empty, the line is not shown to shoppers.

Existing products keep what they have today and show it in the form.

## 2. A new Specifications section

Some products need more detail than Materials & care, for example burn times for a candle that comes in several sizes. Add a **Specifications** section.

- The owner types it in the product form, one bullet per line.
- Shoppers see it on the product page as its own card under Materials & care, with the heading "Specifications".
- A line can contain a link written as `[link text](https://example.com/page)`. It is shown as a link that opens in a new tab. Only `http` and `https` links count; anything else is shown as plain text. No other formatting.
- When a product has no specifications, the card is not shown at all.
- Up to 20 lines, 300 characters each.

Example the owner might type:

```
Scented wax in a glass jar
Small: 40-hour burn time
Large: 85-hour burn time
[Candle care guide](https://example.com/candle-care)
Available in Small and Large, sold separately
```

## 3. Clearer stock messages

At checkout, when a cart asks for more of a product than is in stock, shoppers currently see "Some items are no longer available (Apothecary Balsam Forest Scented Candle: only 8 left). Remove them or lower the quantity in your cart to continue." That reads as if the item is gone, and the cart gives no easy way to lower the quantity.

Change it so that:

- A stock limit says: "Only 8 of Apothecary Balsam Forest Scented Candle are in stock. Lower the quantity to continue."
- An item that is sold out or unpublished keeps a "no longer available" message.
- On the cart page, a line whose quantity is above what is in stock shows how many are available and a button "Change to 8" that sets the quantity.
- The quantity stepper on a cart line stops at what is in stock (and still at the existing maximum of 10).
